## PO diagnosis and next investment decision

Recent research still largely follows the value-bounding architecture inherited
from v2: construct per-pair L/U, then compare intervals. That route is allowed,
but its benefit relative to the weaker ordering objective has not been established.
The next cheap lemma should not be funded merely because it extends the latest
baseline. The reset diagnostic supplied useful evidence, but its elementary
prior-art ingredients already warned that a central contribution was unlikely.

Risk: repeated correct elementary packets can create a long baseline catalog
without closing the scientific-significance gap. Further workflow expansion or
micro-optimization does not resolve this. No additional workflow framework is
needed for this diagnosis.

Before the next construction, make one bounded architecture/investment comparison:

1. A direct order comparison under explicit input-checkable structure, without
   requiring two separately accurate values. This is an open question, not a
   theorem or a promise that cancellation/monotonicity gives a cheap certificate.
2. The currently proposed geometric/approximate-future alignment bound, compared
   against the standard coupling/contraction baseline and including checking cost.

Use the same meaningful finite process class for this comparison. Each proposal
must state: successful paper claim, nearest known result, the actual remaining
mathematical gap, avoided computation and a decisive adversarial case. Prefer the
route whose *success* would be substantive; do not select by which toy formula
is easiest to implement. No registration, new benchmark or broad survey is
authorized automatically by this assessment. If neither route has a credible
contribution, record that and stop the proposed investments rather than add another
low-value variant. This does not close the overall research objective.

Next progress milestone: one independently credible candidate central claim
with a noncircular structural mechanism. Only then fund the decisive probe and
subsequent confirmation. This review changes investment order, not the target,
guarantee family or completion criteria.
